Design Trends 2024: What to Watch

By Mengejar Team | 15 Sep 2026 | Category: Design

2024 is shaping up to be an exciting year for visual design. From bold color palettes to immersive 3D elements, designers are pushing boundaries to capture attention.

1. Neon & Electric Colors

Electric blues, vibrant pinks, and neon greens dominate brand identities, especially in tech and youth‑focused markets.

2. Minimalist 3D

Subtle depth through low‑poly 3D graphics adds a futuristic feel without overwhelming the viewer.

3. Variable Typography

Fonts that adapt weight and width fluidly across breakpoints create dynamic reading experiences.

Integrate these trends into your next project to stay ahead of the curve.
