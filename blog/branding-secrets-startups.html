Branding Secrets for Startups

By Mengejar Team | 1 Sep 2026 | Category: Branding

Building a brand from scratch can feel overwhelming. Here are key steps to create a memorable identity without a huge budget.

1. Define Core Values

Start with why you exist. Your values guide tone, visual style, and messaging.

2. Craft a Simple Logo

Focus on clarity and scalability. A minimalist mark works across platforms.

3. Choose a Consistent Color Palette

Electric blue works well for tech‑focused brands; pair with dark navy for contrast.

4. Voice & Messaging

Maintain a consistent voice—whether bold, friendly, or professional.

Apply these principles and watch your brand gain traction.
