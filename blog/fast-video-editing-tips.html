Fast Video Editing Tips

By Mengejar Team | 20 Aug 2026 | Category: Video Editing

Editing video can be time‑consuming. Here are practical tips to speed up your process while keeping quality high.

1. Organize Your Media

Keep footage in clearly labeled folders. Use proxy files for 4K footage.

2. Use Keyboard Shortcuts

Learn the shortcuts for your editing software; they shave seconds off every action.

3. Leverage Presets & Templates

Color grades, transition presets, and lower‑third templates save repetitive work.

4. Render Only What You Need

Export segments individually during drafts to avoid re‑rendering the whole timeline.

Apply these methods and notice a faster turnaround for your video projects.
